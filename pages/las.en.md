The L.A.S. (Libertas Aurea Silvarum) project has sprouted from a great admiration for the forests of the Tricity Landscape Park. Few places in the world allow for such seamless congregation of sea, forest, and urban environments. The nature eagerly disclosing itself to the city dwellers with such ease is more valuable than gold.

Yet the forests hold many secrets still and vary greatly in their history and the way in which they are used. No forest is likely to survive for long if its elders are not allowed to thrive and to eventually decompose back into the soil that had nurtured them. Thus, our purpose has been to identify and record the oldest, largest trees in the Tricity area, and to petition the authorities to grant these impressive individuals additional protection as natural monuments.

Following the principles of citizen science we have gathered measurements, photographic evidence, biological descriptions, and geographical data. We invite others to do the same. The fruits of our labour we make available for everyone to see.

We dedicate the first 100 trees measured in this effort to the city of Gdynia in particular, on the 100th anniversary of its establishment.
